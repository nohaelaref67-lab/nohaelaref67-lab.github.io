About

Architecture, interiors and exteriors, from concept to visualisation.

Architect and interior designer with a B.Sc. in Architecture from El Shorouk Academy (2022) and professional experience since 2022 across interior and exterior design.

Project experience covers residential building entrances and lobbies, apartment and villa interiors, mall corridors, and commercial and administrative spaces, together with custom furniture design. Projects are taken from concept through 3D modelling and photorealistic visualisation.
